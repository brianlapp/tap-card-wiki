# Competitive Landscape — Kids' Interactive Birthday Cards

**Status:** Draft
**Researched:** 2026-09-20
**Owner:** Brian Lapp
**Method:** Web research only. No pricing pages verified by signup, no user interviews. Treat every figure as "as advertised," not as measured.

---

## TL;DR

Interactive birthday ecards with mini-games already exist and are cheap. Custom-song gifts already exist and are expensive. At least one startup is doing Tapcard's exact concept for free.

Tapcard's defensible angle is **depth of personalization** — a card built from an interview about one specific child — not "interactive card," which is a solved and crowded category.

---

## Tier 1 — Subscription ecard incumbents

Massive libraries, low monthly price, decades of brand trust.

| Company | Price | Relevant to us |
|---|---|---|
| Blue Mountain | $7.99/mo, $35.99/yr, $49.99/2yr | Already ships interactive birthday ecards with mini-games, quizzes and clickable animation; kids' cards personalized by name, incl. singing cards |
| Jacquie Lawson | $8/mo, $36/yr | Hand-drawn animation, original music, some cards include games and puzzles |
| Hallmark+ | $7.99/mo, $79.99/yr | Brand recognition + paper mail integration |
| American Greetings | Membership | SmashUps (name-personalized video), Creatacard |
| JibJab | Membership | Face-swap personalized video cards |
| Doozy | Free/paid | Kids' animated cards incl. a find-the-cake game card |

**Implication:** a $9.99/month Tapcard subscription is priced *above* Blue Mountain while offering a fraction of the library. Subscription is the weak flank.

---

## Tier 2 — Premium personalized gifts

| Company | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Songfinch | ~$199/song | Human musicians; ~1,200 artists, ~100k songs made |
| Songs With You, Tune River, Songheart, Songlorious | ~$199+ | Same model |

Songfinch already partners with American Greetings: the custom song is delivered inside a digital card the recipient opens online.

**Implication:** "card + custom song" is not a new idea. The incumbents own the $199 version. Our advantage would be doing an AI-assisted version at a fraction of the price — but song rights must be verified first (see T-MUSICFLOW-04).

## Tier 3 — Direct competitor ⚠️

**personalized-birthday-games.com** (Ari Gateaux)

- Personalize a free browser game with the child's name, age (8–100) and your message
- One link to share, no app, no postage
- Length adjustable (8–14 cake checkpoints)
- Roadmap: space, wizard-school and pirate themes
- Positioning is nearly identical to ours: a paper card sits in a drawer, this is an adventure they'll remember
- **Price: free**

**Implication:** this is the closest thing to Tapcard found, and it is free. Needs a proper teardown: what's the business model, how good is it on a phone, how deep is the personalization really.

---

## Where the gap is

Everyone above makes you **pick from a library** and drop a name in. Nobody found is building the card *around one child* — their actual interests, their own scenes, their own song, their own mini-games, generated from an adult interview.

That's the bet. It is not yet validated.
