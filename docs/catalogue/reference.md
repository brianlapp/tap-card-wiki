## Where the sections came from: Jordan.EXE map

18 of the 50 sections grow from a part of the reference card (`jordan-exe-birthday.netlify.app`). Component IDs match the build catalogue (`docs/tasks/`).

| Component | Part of Jordan.EXE | Became | What it becomes | What stays the same | What changes per person |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-BOOT | Opening boot | S01 | Boot Gate | Typed lines, the any-key gate, confetti on unlock. | Program name, the 'modules' (their habits and obsessions), the warning line. |
| C-HERO | Birthday opening | S02 S03 | Belated Clock + Name Logo Splash | Cut-out lettering and a hero line that knows today's date. | The lettering style (chosen from their world) and the joke for each date state. |
| C-INC | Incident replay | S06 | Incident Report & Replay | Official-report tone and a replay with counters. | The story (theirs), the root cause and the corrective action. |
| C-PET | Companion scene | S14 S13 | Talking Pet Cameo + Performance Review | Tap, then a line in the pet's voice. Six rows of 'competencies'. | Species, voice and lines. Competencies drawn from real traits. |
| C-GAUGE | Playful diagnostic | S15 | Fake Diagnostic Scan | Sequential rows, rising beeps, a verdict that lands on a joke. | The subject (person or object), the readings and the verdict. |
| C-MUSIC | Mixtape | S37 S38 S39 | Mixtape & Sing-Along Song + Beat Pads + Voice Notes | The cassette player, lyrics highlighted in time, pads and a real voice. | Lyrics, style and tempo, the pad names and who records. |
| C-REPS | Tap challenge | S27 | Tap Challenge | The milestone ladder and the confetti burst. | What is being tapped and every milestone line. |
| C-FUEL | Fill meter | S28 | Fill the Meter | Escalating reactions and a gag reset. | The obsession, its unit and the reactions. |
| C-CHART | Story briefing | S17 | Framework Briefing | A numbered framework and an animated chart. | The framework itself, its parts and the chart axes. |
| C-CERT | Birthday certificate | S18 | Official Decree | Sign, then stamp, then hidden memo. | The clauses, the sections and the memo. |
| C-FIN | Finale and candles | S47 S48 | Launch Sequence + Candle Blowout | Big button, typed lines, cake, candles, then the payoff. | The theme, the lines and the final greeting. |
| C-END | Closing note | S41 S49 | The Letter + Credits & Easter Egg | The handwritten feel, the sign-off and 'refresh to replay'. | The letter itself and the credits list. |

Left out on purpose: adult profanity, the explicit-birthday advisory badge, injury and body jokes, and the five Three.js 3D stages (heavy on phones; the SVG and CSS versions carry the same jokes). Kept as a rule: visuals are inline SVG, CSS or canvas, with no media files apart from real recorded voices.
