# Voice

Big, warm and sure of itself. We write like a friend who knows your person well and doesn't need to shout about it.

## Four traits

| Trait | We are | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm | We say the nice thing plainly. | Greeting-card poetry |
| Bold | Short sentences. Full stops. No shouting. | Caps lock and exclamation marks |
| Specific | Real details beat big adjectives. | "Amazing", "magical", "unforgettable" |
| Playful | We tease like family does. | Jokes about bodies, money or age |

## Six rules

1. Headlines are short and end with a full stop. On screen, the full stop is Tomato.
2. One idea per sentence. Most sentences stay under 15 words.
3. Write "uncard" in lowercase inside sentences. UNCARD in capitals is the logo's job.
4. No exclamation marks in headlines. One per page at most, anywhere.
5. Say "your person", not "the recipient". Say "give", not "send".
6. The person is the headline. The technology never is.

## Words

- **Say:** uncard, scene, world, your person, give, keep, play.
- **Avoid:** e-card, template, AI-generated, content, user, experience, magical.

## Before and after

> **Before:** Create a magical AI-powered personalised e-card experience for your loved ones in minutes!
>
> **After:** Tell us about someone you love. We'll make them a whole little world.

## Beyond birthdays

The same voice covers every new occasion. Swap the occasion, never the tone: "Not a card. A whole little holiday." still ends on a full stop. For sensitive occasions such as sympathy, drop the teasing and keep the warmth and specificity.
