- **Testing and learning plan (Oy, posted by Jordan).** Groundwork for after the build handoff; not a verdict on the current build. Goal: cards that are easy and fun to make, truly personal, great to receive, good value. Seven parts:
  1. Test the whole journey: anonymous visitors, new accounts, paid and subscription states, Golden Pass, admins, recipients; mobile, accessibility, interrupted flows, retries, permissions, credit and cost handling. Golden Pass is unlimited and free, and separate from admin access.
  2. Improve creation and signup from evidence: time to an editable preview, how much personalising people want, where they hesitate or leave. Don't assume more chat or features means better.
  3. Celebrate milestones: first card, first non-birthday occasion, 5 and 10 cards, 3 occasion types. Badges and a congratulations card. System-sent celebration cards must not count toward milestones (no loops).
  4. Free-card offers: one on signup, plus a birthday card with a free card to send each year. Settle eligibility, consent, birthday data and duplicate-grant protection first. Collecting month/day of birth is a hypothesis to test.
  5. Measure both sides separately: the giver's starts, skips, edits, previews and sends, and the recipient's plays, skips, replays, completions and shares. Raw card content stays out of routine analytics.
  6. Easy, optional, dismissible Golden Pass feedback prompts, without nagging.
  7. Social profile connections (LinkedIn/Instagram/Facebook) stay opt-in and future; the pilot uses only what people choose to share.
